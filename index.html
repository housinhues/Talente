This collects the fields the management-agreement reference draws on. It does not draft anything — once both sides finish, submit it and bring the answers back for drafting against the clause library.

Before submitting Confirm the talent's legal name (two different spellings are on file) and check any existing publishing/distribution deal against the exclusivity scope below — per house process, a "No" on other management with an active deal listed is a contradiction to resolve before signing, not draft around.

Fields marked "internal record" are kept on the talent's file, not written into the agreement text, per house process. Compensation (standing commission vs. none) is an open decision — flag your answer clearly, it isn't defaulted.
